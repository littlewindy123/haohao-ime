Each example stores both original sentence IDs and authors. Source links:
https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/{englishId}
https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/{chineseId}
The original texts are not rewritten; they are selected, directly paired and indexed
by whole word/explicit inflection. No audio is included. Selection is not semantic
annotation and does not claim the example matches a user's saved meaning.
Sentence authors, IDs and the license are also shown in the app's example details.
All source checksums, selection rules and the deterministic snapshot are in
app/dictionary/learning in the source repository. Missing examples are not synthesized.
