## 2026-09-08: character keycap atlases (current website)

The earlier landscape avatar images above are historical and excluded from the current publishing whitelist. Six new square 2×2 atlases place the portrait on SPACE, pose on Q, prop on E, and motifs on P and function keys. Letter legends are rendered independently. The fixed front-facing model has all 26 letters and seven functional/punctuation keys. No changes to Android skins or APK.

The user corrected Nailong with a yellow, green-eyed, cream-bellied reference collage, and Cai Xukun with the silver center-part basketball chicken meme; the final atlases follow those references, not the discarded drafts. Original generated PNGs remain in the local Codex generated_images directory; only WebP encodings are published. No composition edits or image cropping were used in conversion. The 2×2 quadrants are mapped at runtime as keyboard textures.

Design skill influence: retain established cream/mint branding, use keycap artwork rather than adjacent large avatars, remove redundant controls and explanation; GSAP timeline is the single hero scheduler. Reduced motion/save-data/WebGL failure retain the same complete board in HTML/CSS.
